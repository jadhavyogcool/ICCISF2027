About the Conference

About ATLAS SkillTech University

ATLAS SkillTech University, located in Mumbai, is a premier institution committed to fostering innovation, creativity, and industry-ready skills. With state-of-the-art infrastructure, world-class faculty, and a vibrant campus culture, ATLAS SkillTech is at the forefront of technology education and research in India. The university offers cutting-edge programs across design, technology, management, and entrepreneurship.

About the Conference

The IEEE 2027 International Conference on Convergent Intelligence for Sustainable Futures (ICCISF 2027) brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and practitioners from around the globe to discuss and share the latest advancements, breakthroughs, and future trends in convergent intelligence technologies and their applications toward building sustainable futures.

Objective

ICCISF 2027 aims to provide a dynamic platform for interdisciplinary dialogue, fostering collaboration between academia and industry. The conference will feature keynote sessions, invited talks, paper presentations, and panel discussions that explore how convergent technologies — AI, IoT, blockchain, data science — can drive sustainability across industries.

Submit

Call for Papers

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished research papers that demonstrate advances in convergent intelligence technologies and their impact on sustainable futures. All submitted papers will be peer-reviewed by the Technical Program Committee.

All accepted and presented papers will be submitted for publication in IEEE Xplore Digital Library (subject to IEEE review)
Papers must be formatted using the standard IEEE conference template
Maximum paper length: 6 pages (including references)
Submissions must be original work not previously published or under review
